# Agent instructions: V-LUP fan-game guidelines

Before making **each** project change, read `VLUP_GUIDELINE.md` and compare the proposed change with its applicable requirements. Do this for code, copy, illustrations and other media, assets, metadata, hosting or deployment configuration, and project documentation. Do not rely on memory from an earlier task; the guideline may have changed.

## Required review

- Keep this project non-commercial. Do not add sales, paid downloads, in-app purchases, paid beta access, donation or subscription rewards, sponsorships, affiliate banners, link-shortener ads, or ad-supported hosting/distribution.
- Keep content within the stated restrictions: no harassment, sexual content, political/religious/ideological satire, antisocial content, or privacy invasion, stalking, or impersonation involving a streamer.
- Do not extract or redistribute original high-resolution artwork, photocards, or source audio, and do not provide a near-identical copy of an original work as a substitute.
- Label AI-generated content. Do not use V-LUP or creator-owned works as training data. Follow the stated limits for voice synthesis; do not add voice synthesis without reviewing the current guideline and the exact character and use case.
- Keep the required credit `Original By Creatorbus Inc.` and cite the official source link for any original song or content actually used. Keep the project's separate SITRY outfit-reference VOD and official station attribution accurate.
- Preserve the distinction between this project's fan-made assets and official artwork. Use the established display names `시트리` and `차미`; `SITRY` is acceptable as decorative design text. Do not invent an official English name for 차미.

## Keep disclosures and documents in sync

When a change adds, removes, or materially changes content, assets, AI use, audio, or distribution behavior, update all affected disclosures in the same change: the site's `이용 가이드` dialog in `src/main.js`, the project AI inventory in `VLUP_GUIDELINE.md`, and the relevant README sections. In particular, update the AI O/X entries whenever the actual use changes. Do not mark a capability unused if the project now uses it.

Keep the supplied V-LUP guideline text in `VLUP_GUIDELINE.md` intact as the source copy. Record project-specific interpretations, references, or AI disclosures in separate sections so they cannot be mistaken for V-LUP's original text. If the official source appears to have changed, verify it before revising the project summary and identify the update; do not silently rewrite the supplied source text.

Before finishing each task, make a brief guideline review: identify the affected requirements, check the resulting change against them, and synchronize any required disclosures or documentation. If a requested change conflicts with the guideline, do not implement the conflicting part; explain the conflict and offer a compatible alternative. Do not describe this review as legal approval or guaranteed compliance.

## Public release reminder

The project documentation and on-site guide do not provide permission or replace the required advance notice. Before any public release, the project owner must verify the current official guideline, notify V-LUP with the distribution link and description, and use an allowed distribution channel. Do not publish or deploy publicly on the owner's behalf unless explicitly asked and authorized.
